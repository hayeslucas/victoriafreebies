Our vision

Victoria Freebies makes giving easier. People give their items to people in need, and this guide removes the guesswork for donors and the extra sorting for charities.

Goals

  • One simple place to check where an item can be donated
  • Fewer unwanted drop-offs for charities
  • Clear, current information with a source and date on every entry

Timeline

Start small

Now: a searchable guide to local charities’ wishlists and donation rules. Some are posted online, others confirmed by contacting charities directly.

Slowly grow

Add more charities, more areas and more item categories, guided by community feedback.

The dream

Let individuals post requests for items they need, so neighbours can help neighbours in one trusted place.
