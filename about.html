Our mission

Victoria Freebies connects people who have something to give with the local charities that need it. We show donors exactly what’s needed and where to bring it, so donations reach the people they’re meant for and charities spend less time sorting what they can’t use.

Goals

  • One place to find where any item can be donated
  • Less time spent coordinating donations for charities
  • Good items kept out of the landfill and put to use where they’re needed

Our vision

Start small

Now: a searchable guide to local charities’ wishlists and donation rules. Some are posted online, others confirmed by contacting charities directly.

Slowly grow

Add more charities, more areas and more item categories, guided by community feedback.

The dream

Let individuals post requests for items they need, so neighbours can help neighbours in one trusted place.
