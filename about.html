Our mission

Victoria Freebies makes sure good things reach the people who need them. We show donors exactly what local charities are asking for and where to bring it, so every donation helps someone, charities spend less time sorting what they can’t use, and less ends up in the landfill.

Where we’re headed

Start small

Now: a searchable guide to what local charities need and how to give it.

Slowly grow

Add more charities, more areas and more item categories, guided by community feedback.

The dream

Let individuals post requests for items they need, so neighbours can help neighbours in one trusted place.
