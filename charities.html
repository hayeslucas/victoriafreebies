Why we built this

You do important work, but coordinating in-kind donations takes up a lot of your time. We want to work with you to make it simpler, so more of what arrives is what you actually asked for.

What you can do with the form

  • Add a need. List the items you’re looking for and how donors should get them to you.
  • Update a listing. Change the items, drop-off address, hours or phone number.
  • Take a listing down. When you have enough, or no longer need something.

We review every submission before it goes on the site.
