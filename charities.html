For charities

You do important work. Managing in-kind donations shouldn’t take up so much of your time.

We want to make in-kind giving easier for you

Charities across Greater Victoria do essential work for people in our community. But coordinating in-kind donations takes a lot of time: answering calls about what you accept, sorting through items you can’t use, and turning away drop-offs you don’t have room for.

We want to work with you to streamline that process. Victoria Freebies shows donors what you need right now and how to get it to you, so more of what arrives is what you asked for, and less of your time goes to managing what you didn’t.

How it works
